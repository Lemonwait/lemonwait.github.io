eHP

Why do some figures go up when enemies hit harder?

eHP is the raw enemy ATK an operator takes before she falls. For most operators it goes down as hits get bigger, because DEF cuts less of a big hit. A few have protection that lasts a number of hits or seconds instead, whatever each hit's size, so bigger hits add up to more ATK taken:

  • Cement S2: 20 stacks of DEF, one used per hit; she lasts about 20 hits, small or big.
  • Morgan S2: her Barrier fades on its own over 6s; big hits use it up before it fades, small hits leave it to fade.
  • Lessing (IDEAL): S2 cuts almost all damage for 24s, so the skill runs out before he does.

Operators who survive the fight can go up too: their figure adds what they could still take at the next hit's size.
