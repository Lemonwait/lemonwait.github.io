    FAQ

    ✦ Frequently Asked Questions

    What should I do first?

    Whatever you want. There's a way to rush the game if you love going fast — but time beats all. Two years of 50% efficiency will always win over one month of unhappy min-maxing.

    Who should I pull?

    Whoever you want. The game lets you borrow one unit from your friendlist, so don't feel bad about borrowing their strength — it only gets specific when you go absolute high-end. If you're not a fan of 2D RPG combat, look for the modern AFK 6★ units.

    Strongest units?

    For a rough overview, open puppiiz and click Grouped — it sorts Operators by release order. Later generally means stronger, bar most Free 6★ Operators. It's a swing of a metric, but the game has simplified itself with respectable powercreep. It's up to you how you'd use that.

    Team Building?

    DP, Tank, Heal, AoE are enough for general content, especially when you lean on friend supports. As your roster grows, so does your control of the stage — and it won't take long, since Arknights drowns you in off-rate 6★s. The downside: chasing one specific 6★ can mean 200–300 pulls.

    Resource allocation follows the same principle as other gachas, with extra steps. Don't pour everything into one unit — the core is DP, Tank, Heal, AoE. The magic number is 30: stop levelling around Lv. 30–40 until you can auto-farm, then promote and repeat.
